# Microsoft Teams

Notifications as a card in a Teams channel through a workflow.

## Setup

1. In the Teams channel open **Workflows** and choose the template **Post to a channel when a webhook request is received**.
2. Pick team and channel and save the workflow.
3. Enter the URL shown here. It contains a signature and acts like a password: SerMon stores it encrypted and never shows it again.

Microsoft retires the old Office 365 connectors; use the workflow.

## Appearance

The card shows the title in the colour of the level, the values of the alert and a button to the node in SerMon. Values of the servers appear as plain text, without Markdown.
