# Anomalies

Which nodes behave unusually right now and what stood out in the last hour.

## Nodes by anomaly rate

The table sorts the nodes by their current [anomaly rate](help:metrics/sermon_anomaly_rate_ratio), next to it the
highest of the last 15 minutes. From 10 % it says "high". Nodes in their [learning phase](help:concepts/anomalies) are
missing. A click opens the node's page with the anomaly band in the charts.

## What was unusual?

The metrics of the whole fleet with the highest score of the last hour, with node, anomalous minutes and the time of
the highest value. The page refreshes every minute.
