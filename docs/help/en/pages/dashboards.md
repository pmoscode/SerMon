# Dashboards

Your own dashboards next to the built-in views: put the charts, values, log lines and alerts you need side by side,
keep them private or share them with the organization.

## Using it

- **New:** give a title and create the dashboard; it opens in the editor. It is private until you share it.
- **Shared** dashboards are seen by every member of the organization. Each widget shows only the data of the nodes the
  viewer may see – sharing gives nobody new rights.
- **Changing:** the owner changes, shares and deletes a dashboard; a shared one also an admin of all groups. Admins do
  not see private dashboards of others.
- **Import:** choose the JSON file of an exported dashboard (at most 32 KiB). It becomes your private dashboard. Nodes
  and groups it names must exist in this organization and be visible to you – a file of another instance or
  organization is rejected; [variables](help:pages/dashboard) make dashboards portable.

At most 200 dashboards per organization.
